A collection of scripts to sync my school's tasks
(from [Canvas](https://www.instructure.com/canvas) and
[Gradescope](https://www.gradescope.com/)) to my personal
[CalDAV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CalDAV) server. I didn't want to look at
three websites (Canvas, Gradescope, and my TODO app) in order to figure out what
to do, and instead just look at my TODO apps.

[Source code](https://github.com/nyiyui/caldav-canvas-gradescope)

This project was partially vibecoded (specifically `canvas.py` and `caldav_sync.py`).
